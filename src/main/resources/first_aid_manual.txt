# First Aid Manual and Emergency Protocols

## 1. Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation (CPR)
If the patient is unresponsive and not breathing normally:
1. Call emergency services immediately.
2. Place the heel of one hand on the center of the chest, and the other hand on top.
3. Push hard and fast (at least 2 inches deep, 100-120 compressions per minute).
4. Allow the chest to recoil completely between compressions.
5. If trained, provide 2 rescue breaths after every 30 compressions.
6. Continue until paramedics arrive or an AED is available.

## 2. Heart Attack (Myocardial Infarction)
Symptoms: Crushing chest pain, shortness of breath, pain radiating to arm or jaw, sweating.
Protocol:
1. Have the patient sit down, rest, and try to keep calm.
2. Loosen any tight clothing.
3. Ask if they take any chest pain medication, such as nitroglycerin, for a known heart condition, and help them take it.
4. If the pain does not go away promptly with rest or within 3 minutes of taking nitroglycerin, call for emergency medical help.
5. If the person is unconscious and unresponsive, begin CPR.

## 3. Severe Bleeding
Protocol:
1. Apply firm, direct pressure to the wound using a clean cloth or sterile bandage.
2. If blood soaks through, do not remove the cloth; add another one on top.
3. If the bleeding is from a limb, elevate it above the heart if possible.
4. If bleeding is life-threatening and cannot be controlled with direct pressure, apply a tourniquet 2-3 inches above the wound (not on a joint).
5. Seek immediate medical attention.

## 4. Stroke
Remember the FAST test:
- **F**ace drooping: Does one side of the face droop or is it numb?
- **A**rm weakness: Is one arm weak or numb? Ask the person to raise both arms.
- **S**peech difficulty: Is speech slurred?
- **T**ime to call 911: If someone shows any of these symptoms, call emergency services immediately.
Do not give the person anything to eat or drink. Keep them comfortable.
